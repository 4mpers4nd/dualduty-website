Privacy policy.
The working draft.

Draft · October 18, 2026. Describes this pre-launch static website. Not legally reviewed or a compliance certification.

What this website does

Dual Duty Junk Removal is a pre-launch local business based in Spring City, Utah. The website describes planned services and provides a browser-based load planner. It does not currently accept quote requests, bookings, payments, or photo uploads.

Calculator storage on your device

The planner saves selected catalog items, quantities, editable volumes, loading difficulty, stair count, service community, and a special-handling flag in this browser’s localStorage under dualDuty.plan.v1. It does not request names, street addresses, contact details, dates, free-text notes, or photos. Data remains until you choose “Clear saved plan” or clear this site’s browser data. It is not sent to the business. Other users of the same browser profile may be able to see it.

Information we do not collect through forms

The contact form is a disabled preview. Fields cannot be edited, files cannot be selected, and no form submissions are made. The website includes no advertising trackers, analytics scripts, third-party fonts, or tracking cookies.

Hosting and technical requests

Your browser requests website files from the hosting provider. Hosting providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request times, and browser details for delivery, security, and operations. For the intended GitHub Pages deployment, consult GitHub’s privacy statement. Hosting preview environments may have their own policies.

Copying, downloading, and printing

These actions happen on your device. Copying writes the non-personal plan summary to your clipboard only when you request it. Downloaded or printed copies are under your control. Clearing the planner does not remove downloaded files or clipboard contents.

External links

Map source links and documentation may lead to other websites. Those websites operate under their own policies. No external map tiles or geographic APIs load automatically.

Future changes

Before adding customer requests, scheduling, payment services, or analytics, this policy must be updated to identify providers, data collected, purposes, retention practices, and available privacy controls. Existing feature switches do not activate data collection by themselves.

Questions

Business contact information is not yet configured. A privacy contact method will be provided on the contact page before the business begins collecting customer information online.
