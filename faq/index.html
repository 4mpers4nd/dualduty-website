What’s planned, what’s confirmed, and what we’re still working out.

LaunchAre you taking bookings yet?

Not yet. Dual Duty Junk Removal is in the planning stage. Online requests, scheduling, and payments are not available. A launch date has not been announced.

ServicesWhat are you planning to take?

General household junk, furniture, garage and basement clutter, moving cleanouts, and some yard debris are planned. Appliance, residential debris, and light commercial work require confirmation before launch.

PricingHow much will a pickup cost?

Company rates are not set. The load planner estimates volume and flags complexity without showing invented prices. Final prices will depend on the actual items, access, labor, travel, weight, and disposal costs.

SchedulingCan I reserve a date?

Scheduling is not open. The contact form is a disabled preview; entering a preferred date is not currently possible and does not reserve service.

MaterialsWhat materials are excluded?

We do not plan to accept hazardous waste, asbestos, chemicals, fuels, paint liquids, explosives, medical waste, or unidentified substances. Items requiring specialist handling must be discussed before any future pickup.

MaterialsCan you take refrigerators or mattresses?

These are included in the planner for estimating space, not as guaranteed accepted items. Refrigerant handling and mattress disposal arrangements must be confirmed.

AreasWhere do you plan to operate?

Our operating base is Spring City. We plan to cover Sanpete County, including the 13 listed communities and surrounding rural areas. Exact address coverage and travel charges will need confirmation once inquiries open.

DisposalWill everything be recycled or donated?

No blanket promise. Our intention is to consider reuse, donation, and recycling where item condition, receiving facilities, and practical arrangements allow. No disposal partnerships are confirmed.

PreparationHow should I prepare for a future pickup?

Identify only the items you want removed, keep belongings you want to retain separate, and plan a clear access route. Do not move heavy objects or disconnect utilities for the sake of an estimate. Specific instructions will be confirmed with the job.

Heavy itemsCan you remove very heavy items?

Weight and safe handling limits are still being determined. Pianos, safes, hot tubs, large machinery, concrete, and other unusually heavy items need individual approval and may be declined.

SchedulingDo you offer same-day service?

No same-day service or response time is promised. Availability and hours will be published when operations are ready.

PoliciesWhat is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation and rescheduling policies are still being finalized. They will be provided before customers agree to a booking.

PaymentsWhich payment methods do you accept?

Payment methods are not configured. This website does not request deposits or process payments. Accepted methods and payment timing will be published before launch.

PrivacyDoes the calculator send you my information?

No. The calculator works in your browser and saves only item selections and job factors on this device. It does not ask for a name, street address, email, phone number, or photos. Clear saved plan removes its stored data.

ServicesAre demolition and hazardous waste services available?

No. Demolition, hazardous waste disposal, and specialized removal services are outside the planned offering.

PricingIs the load planner a binding quote?

No. Item volumes are editable planning assumptions, not measurements. Trailer capacity and weight limits are not yet confirmed. Even after real rates are configured, any price is preliminary and requires business confirmation.
